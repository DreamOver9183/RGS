feat(home): add rating, price, open-now, and type filters

Add a filter sheet next to Home's radius with a minimum rating, price
levels, an open-now switch, and four restaurant categories. Categories
become the nearby search's includedPrimaryTypes; rating, price, and
open-now request the Enterprise filter fields only while one of them is
on, and shops missing the data are excluded. The filter is saved in
DataStore (filter_v1) and applies only to Home's draw.
